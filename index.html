Breakfast food is overrated

Even though its the first meal of the day, breakfast food is not as good as other meals.

Limited meals compared to other meals

Breakfast sticks to the same few ingredients like eggs, toast, and bacon, offering way less variety, spice, and global flavor than lunch or dinner.

The food does not taste as good as other meals

Morning food is usually bland or masked in sugar, featuring mostly mushy textures and basic flavors that cannot match the rich, savory depth of evening dishes.

Breakfast food is expensive

Quality protein sources like eggs, bacon, and specialized coffee can inflate a grocery budget or morning spending quickly compared to a simple lunch and dinner plan.
